# PhreakNIC-CCG — Rules clarifications (designer rulings, 2026-11-10)

Eighteen ambiguous rules / card interactions were raised against the current engine
(`scripts/core/rules.gd`, `scripts/core/effects.gd`) and the written rules
(`reference/rules.md`, errata v1.1) across three rounds of questions. The designer has now
ruled on all eighteen. This document records each ruling, what the engine does **today**,
and whether a code change is required.

**Summary:** 16 of 18 already match the engine. **One confirmed bug** (#7, *I've got the
Logs*) needs a fix, and there is **one minor refinement** worth tidying (#10, Hero-buy
destination). All follow-up questions have been answered — see "Follow-up rulings"
(#12–18) near the end.

## Confirmed rulings

### 1. Extra turns re-run every "beginning of your turn" effect — *intended*
Time Travel and TOCTOU Photo-Finish both grant a full extra turn, and the engine runs a
complete start-of-turn on it (`reaction_turn` / the `extra_turns` loop both call
`start_turn`, which calls `effects.start_turn`).

Consequences, all **intended**:
- **The Juice** adds another counter and deals again — a Time Travel extra turn can push it
  to the 5th counter and make the controller lose mid-combo.
- **Clarion Inn Air Conditioning** drains 1 life from every player again.
- **Kevin's Desk** steals again; **pTFS** returns heroes to hand again; **The Book**
  attaches + replays again; a **Rickroll** in the Center Row forces another buy.

No change. (Suggest adding a one-line note to `reference/rules.md` under the Timing or
Rulings section so players know extra turns are "real" turns for all start-of-turn triggers.)

### 2. O.P.R.A.H. — drawing from the center deck counts as *acquiring*
"Remove any number of cards in your hand from the game. Draw that many cards from the
center deck." Pulling those cards **is acquiring**. Therefore playing O.P.R.A.H.:
- sets the "acquired a card this turn" flag,
- **disables Zenthere, Mind Goblin's** end-of-turn bonus,
- **forbids reserving** that turn,
- satisfies / breaks any "if you acquire … this turn" check.

Engine already routes O.P.R.A.H. through `acquire()`. No change. (Worth adding O.P.R.A.H.
to the "Acquire" examples list in `reference/rules.md` §"Buying vs. acquiring", which
currently omits it.)

### 3. The Book — attached-Action behavior is correct as implemented
- Attaching is mandatory at the start of your turn while you hold an Action.
- Attached Actions that do **not** move themselves stay attached and **replay every turn**
  (a persistent {H}/{D}/draw engine). Intended.
- An attached Action that is **illegal that turn** (e.g. Nirtom Unleashed's "no other
  Actions" clause, Even More Logo vs. _NSAKEY, PhreakMyst after a buy) is **skipped for
  that turn but stays attached** to retry later.

No change. (This is already consistent with Ruling 19.)

### 4. "Choose and discard a permanent in play" may target opponents
Nuvious (on buy) and Still Not Spock let the controller pick **any** permanent in play —
including an opponent's — and send it to its controller's discard. This follows Ruling 14
("in play" with no owner named = any side). Intended; no change.

> Contrast (unchanged, already clear): AI Slop and Talks Fun Police make **each player**
> discard one of their **own** permanents; the engine does this per-player.

### 5. Botnet Takeover scoops up *all* Botnets
"If there are any permanents named 'Botnet' in play, put them into your discard pile."
This moves **every** Botnet on the table — any player's — into the caster's discard
(and only deals 2 {D} if there are none). Confirmed intended. No change.

### 6. Etch-a-Sketch Skillz copying a card that refers to itself → **remove Etch**
When the copied effect references the card by name or as "this card" (e.g. **Jenny** —
"…then remove Jenny from the game"), the reference resolves to **Etch-a-Sketch Skillz**,
and it is Etch that leaves the game.

Engine already does this: copied effects run on a *virtual* instance whose `flags.host`
points at the real Etch, and `move()` / `remove()` redirect virtual → host
(`rules.gd:108-135`). So `_effect_jenny`'s `e.remove(c)` removes Etch. No change.
Generalize the principle in `reference/rules.md` Ruling 9: *any* self-reference in copied
text (printed name or "this card") = Etch.

### 7. I've got the Logs — **per-revealer damage only** ⚠️ CODE CHANGE
Card: "Name a card. Each opponent reveals their hand. If any player reveals the named
card, deal {D} equal to that card's cost, and remove this from the game. Otherwise, draw a
card."

**Ruling:** the damage goes to the opponents who **revealed** the named card — each such
opponent takes {D} equal to the named card's (printed) cost. Opponents who did **not**
reveal it take nothing.

**Engine today is wrong** (`effects.gd:513`, `_effect_i_ve_got_the_logs`): it sets a single
`hit` flag and calls `e.deal(p, cost)`, which deals to **all** opponents, not just the
revealers.

**Fix spec:**
- For each opponent, reveal their hand; if that opponent reveals the named card, deal
  `cost` damage **to that opponent specifically** (use the targeted `damage(p, opponent,
  cost, c)` path, not `deal(...)` which hits all opponents).
- If **at least one** opponent revealed it, remove I've got the Logs from the game.
- If **no** opponent revealed it, draw a card (unchanged).
- Printed cost per Ruling 10 (ignore discounts) — unchanged.

> Multi-reveal detail confirmed in #14: two revealers → two separate hits; remove the card
> if at least one opponent revealed it.

### 8. Military-Grade Card Encapsulation + ICE — owner chooses, ICE need not drain
With both MGCE ("you take no damage") and an ICE Location in play, the defender is **not
forced to spend ICE counters**. When several sources could prevent the same damage, the
**owner of the prevention sources chooses** which source prevents each point — so they can
route all damage into MGCE and keep their ICE counters.

Engine already models this: `resolve_damage` (`rules.gd:321-363`) builds the prevention
`sources` list (MGCE/Phreaknic Network as "full" guards + each ICE Location) and calls
`allocate_prevention` for the defender to split the points. A "full" guard like MGCE
absorbs without losing counters and stays in play. No change.

### 9. Treasure Hunters — a "whiff" still spends the {H}
Treasure Hunters pays the chosen {H} up front; if **no** Center-Deck card has that exact
cost, nothing is acquired and the {H} is **lost** (then the deck is shuffled). Confirmed.
Engine matches (`_effect_treasure_hunters`). No change.

### 10. Multiple "where does the bought Hero go" redirects — controller picks one ⚠️ MINOR REFINE
With The Fellowship of the Token Ring (→ hand) and/or pTFS (→ onto pTFS) in play, buying a
Hero that **also** redirects itself (e.g. **Fore04** → top of deck) produces competing
destinations. **Ruling:** the controller picks exactly **one** destination.

Engine (`effects.gd:1039 bought()` / `1054 bought_self()`): the simultaneous triggers are
ordered by the active player (good), and Fellowship/pTFS share a `destination.chosen` flag
so only one of *those two* applies. **However, Fore04's self-move in `bought_self()` is
not gated by that flag**, so Fore04 + Fellowship can both run and the second one to resolve
silently overrides the first. In practice the player still lands the card where they want
(they order the triggers and answer the "you may" prompts), so this is **cosmetic/robustness**,
not a rules error.

**Suggested refinement:** have `bought_self()`'s Fore04 branch respect the same
`destination.chosen` flag (and set it when it moves the card), so exactly one redirect fires
and the prompts can't contradict each other.

### 11. Defensive ICE-counter abilities work on your own turn only
Candy Factory, The Sandwich Factory, and Tennessee Dept. of Health abilities (add/remove
ICE counters) are usable **only on the controller's own turn**. You cannot pump ICE in
response to an incoming attack on an opponent's turn. Confirmed intended; engine matches
(`abilities()` gates on `e.is_turn(p)`). No change.

## Follow-up rulings (resolved)

These three questions from the first round have now been answered. All three **already
match the engine** — no additional code changes.

### 12. The Juice changing control (Kevin's Desk / AI Fingers) — the *new* owner takes it
If a player gains control of an opponent's The Juice, the **counters travel with the card**
and the **new controller** is the one who ticks it. So stealing a 4-counter Juice means it
ticks to the 5th counter at the start of the new controller's turn and **the new controller
loses the game**.

Engine matches: `take_control` (`rules.gd:136`) sets `card.controller = p.seat` and
`move()` preserves `counters`; the start-of-turn trigger fires for the current controller
and `eliminate()` targets that same player. No change.

> Design consequence: gaining control of a near-full Juice is a liability, not a gift —
> and Kevin's Desk is mandatory (Ruling 21), though it lets you pick a permanent you
> already control, so you can avoid being forced to grab an enemy Juice.

### 13. Nashville 2600 — "all other permanents" / "cannot play permanents" include yourself
Both clauses apply to **every** player, the Nashville controller included: playing it
discards your **own** board as well as every opponent's (everything except Nashville
itself), and **no one** — you included — may play permanents while it is in play.

Engine matches: `_effect_nashville_2600` (`effects.gd:623`) calls `leave_play` on every
permanent except itself across all play areas, and the permanent-play ban in
`legal_effect` (`rules.gd:180`) is not scoped to a seat. No change.

### 14. I've got the Logs, multi-reveal (confirms the #7 fix spec)
In a 3–4-player game: each opponent who reveals the named card takes a **separate** instance
of {D} = the card's printed cost (two revealers → two hits), and the card is removed from
the game as long as **at least one** opponent revealed it. This is exactly the behavior the
#7 fix specifies.

### 15. ICE counters mandatorily absorb *every* "lose life" effect — intended
Because losing life is treated as taking damage (errata) and absorption is mandatory
(Ruling 2), **all** ambient life loss is forced through a defender's ICE, with **no option
to decline** and take the life instead. Confirmed intended. Consequences:
- **Clarion Inn Air Conditioning** removes one counter from the player's own ICE Location
  every turn (the controller's turn included).
- **r0nk** ("each player loses 1 life, including yourself") spends one of *your* counters.
- **Powered-On Taser Maintenance** (buy penalty), **Unattended Phone** (self-loss),
  **The Juice**, and **F.O.T.T.R. Greatest Hits Volume 2** all absorb the same way.

Engine matches by construction: life is decremented in exactly one place
(`rules.gd:366`, inside `resolve_damage`), which is the ICE-absorbing path; `simultaneous_loss`
and every `deal`/`damage` funnel through it. No change. (Worth a one-line note in
`reference/rules.md` that "lose life" is absorbed by ICE like any damage, since it means
ICE Locations passively bleed counters.)

### 16. TOCTOU Photo-Finish inside a simultaneous elimination — breaks the draw, and nests
- **(a)** If every remaining player would be eliminated at the same time (Ruling 7 → draw),
  a player holding TOCTOU Photo-Finish **is pulled out first** (sets life to 1, takes the
  extra turn) and can end up the **sole survivor, winning instead of drawing**.
- **(b)** Chained interrupts (player A's TOCTOU extra turn deals lethal to player B, who
  also holds TOCTOU) **nest recursively**, each returning to the paused turn in order.

Engine matches: `check_win()` is suppressed while `simultaneous_depth > 0` (`rules.gd:430`),
so the draw/win decision is deferred until the whole simultaneous group resolves; within the
group each `eliminate()` individually offers TOCTOU (`rules.gd:371-378`), and `reaction_turn`
saves/restores turn state so interrupts nest. No change.

### 17. Clarion Inn Air Conditioning is symmetric — intended
"At the beginning of each player's turn, that player loses 1 life" applies to **every**
player, the controller included; the controller loses 1 life on their own turn too. Engine
matches (`start_trigger` runs the A/C branch for all players). No change.

### 18. What is Truth? + I've got the Logs — always a hit, intended
Because What is Truth? "is considered to have all card names" (Ruling 12), a player holding
it always counts as revealing the card named by I've got the Logs, so they **always** take
{D} equal to that card's cost from any opponent's Logs. Confirmed intended; this is a live
consequence of the per-revealer fix in #7. No change (Ruling 12 already covers it).
