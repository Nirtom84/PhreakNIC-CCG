[h1]FOTTR TCG: PhreakNIC Game Rules[/h1]

[h2]Setup[/h2]
- Each player shuffles a personal starting deck of [b]8 Hacker[/b] cards and [b]2 Attack[/b] cards.
- Start with [b]24 life in a 2-player game[/b], or [b]10 life per player[/b] with 3–4 players. Starting life can be agreed before play.
- The [b]Center Deck[/b] contains the base set’s [b]102 cards[/b] in a Base Set · PhreakNIC 26 game, or [b]150 cards[/b] with Expansion 1 · PhreakNIC 27; shuffle it before playing.
- Deal the top 5 cards of the Center Deck face up. This row is the [b]Center Row[/b].
- Each player draws 5 cards from their deck as a starting hand.
- Choose the first player at random, then follow seat order.

[h2]Your Turn[/h2]
- Play any number of cards from your hand, in any order. Action cards go to your discard pile right after they are played.
- [b]Permanent[/b] cards stay in play in your area until something removes them.
- [b]Damage[/b] {D} reduces the life of [b]all other players[/b] by the amount dealt.
- [b]Hacking[/b] {H} is spent to buy cards from the Center Row. A card's cost is the number in its upper-left corner. Cards you buy go to your discard pile.
- Whenever a card leaves the Center Row, it is replaced right away with the top card of the Center Deck.
- At the end of your turn, discard any cards left in your hand and draw 5 new cards. Unspent hacking is lost. Play passes to the next player.

[h2]Drawing, Life and Winning[/h2]
- If you need to draw a card and your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile to form a new deck.
- A player with zero life is out. Remove their permanents from the game and return their reserved cards to the bottom of the Center Deck. Fill any empty Center Row slots right away.
- If everyone is eliminated at the same time, the game is a draw.
- The last player still alive wins.

[h2]Reserving a Card[/h2]
- Instead of buying or otherwise acquiring anything on your turn, you may [b]reserve[/b] one card: take it from the Center Row and set it sideways in your play area.
- Only you may buy a card you reserved. It stays in your area until you buy it.
- A reserved card has not been bought and does nothing until it is. It is not "in play".
- Reserving a card does not count as buying or acquiring a card.

[h2]Additional Rules[/h2]
- If several cards would prevent damage to you, you choose how to split each point among them, including ICE and cards that prevent all damage. You must use available prevention before losing life.
- Extra turns (Time Travel, TOCTOU Photo-Finish) are full turns: repeat every beginning-of-turn effect.
- Acquiring (including O.P.R.A.H. Center Deck draws) counts against reserving and Zenthere, but does not trigger buy effects.
- In copied text, any self-reference (printed name or "this card") means Etch-a-Sketch Skillz.
- Competing bought-Hero destinations (Fore04, Fellowship, pTFS): the controller chooses exactly one.
- When effects happen at the same time, the active player chooses their order. This includes start-of-turn effects and buying Rickroll. A buying prohibition blocks that buy.
- The card printed [b]_NASKEY[/b] counts as [b]_NSAKEY[/b] for card-name checks.
- Losing life is the same as taking damage, including mandatory ICE absorption; you cannot choose to lose life instead.
- [b]ICE counters[/b]: when you would take damage, each point removes one counter from your location instead. Damage beyond the counters left goes through to your life.

[h2]Playing This Version[/h2]
• Party leaders may ban market cards before play, removing all copies. Starter cards stay; at least 40 center-deck cards must remain before the five-card row is dealt. View bans beside the center deck.
• Online hosts can close an unstarted room for everyone. A disconnected host has 60 seconds to reconnect before the room closes.
- Online turns warn after 2 minutes idle; after 3 minutes a bot finishes your turn. Three idle turns in a row give the bot full control. Prompts keep their 45-second fallback.
Choose 2–4 players, bot difficulty, card set and Standard or custom starting life. DEFCON bots each start with twice the chosen starting life. They follow the same rules and have no hidden information. Base Set · PhreakNIC 26 has 102 cards; Base Set + Expansion 1 · PhreakNIC 27 has 150. Mog Variant · PhreakNIC 27 also has 150, replacing cards with their Mog versions.
Tap a market or reserved card for its detail sheet and Buy or Reserve. Tap hand cards to play immediately. Right-click, long-press or tap a Network Log link to read a card. Close, tap outside or press Back to dismiss the sheet.
Play Basics plays only Hacker and Attack; Play All plays every legal card. End Turn asks for confirmation while moves remain; change this in Options. Auto-end never asks.
When prevention offers a choice, use − and + to split all required absorption, then Confirm. Report a bug opens a prefilled GitHub form from either menu.

- [b]Play online[/b] browses public games or creates a room for friends. Share its invite link or join by code. Leave game keeps your seat for [b]Resume online game[/b]; the server saves online progress.
- In a web browser, saves and settings are stored in that browser. Hold phones sideways to play.
- Permanents with abilities show buttons under them.
- The [b]IN EFFECT[/b] strip above the Center Row lists cards that change the rules for everyone, such as Nashville 2600. Tap a warning to read the card and see what you can do about it.
- Hover over cards or Network Log card names on desktop to see a larger image.
- Drag to scroll on touch screens.
- When you have nothing left to do, your turn ends by itself after a moment. Turn [b]Auto-end turn[/b] off on the main menu or in the in-game menu if you prefer to press End Turn yourself.
- [b]Menu[/b] (or Back / Escape) opens the in-game menu with options, Rules, Save game, Main menu and Exit.
- The game saves automatically at the start of each of your turns and when you leave. [b]Resume game[/b] on the main menu continues your saved game.
- On the main menu, type your handle or press [b]Pick a Hero[/b] to play as one of the Hero cards; their art becomes your avatar.
- This game follows the official rules card, the official errata v1.1 (tcg.fottr.io) and the designer's rulings, compiled by bitwize.
